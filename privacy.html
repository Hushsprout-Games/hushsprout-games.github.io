隱私權政策

遊戲在你的電腦上做什麼

  • 只算按了幾下。遊戲會計算鍵盤按鍵和手把按鈕的總次數，讓你打字時角色得到回饋。它不讀取、不儲存你打了什麼，不記錄是哪個鍵、按鍵順序或每次按鍵的時間，也不知道你正在用哪個程式。方向鍵不計，按住不放只算一次。
  • 全螢幕判斷。為了在你開全螢幕程式時讓開，遊戲每 0.5 秒只確認「目前的視窗是否蓋滿整個螢幕」。不讀取視窗標題、程式名稱，結果也不會存下來。
  • 其他聲音時暫停音樂（可在設定關閉）。遊戲只確認「過去一秒有沒有其他程式發出聲音」，用來暫停背景音樂。不錄音，不讀取聲音內容或程式名稱。
  • 開機自動啟動（預設關閉）。只有在你打開這個設定時，才會在 Windows 的開機啟動清單加入本遊戲；關掉就移除。
  • 存檔留在你的電腦。遊戲進度和設定存在 %APPDATA%\TaskbarMiracles，內容只有遊戲狀態和按鍵總數，沒有你打的文字。
  • 沒有帳號、沒有追蹤。遊戲不需要註冊帳號，不含分析或遙測工具，也不會把你的資料傳給我們。

Steam

遊戲透過 Steam 發行，成就由 Steam 處理。你在 Steam 上的帳號與購買資料由 Valve 依 Steam 隱私權政策處理，我們不會取得你的 Steam 帳號資料。

Facebook、Instagram、Threads 與廣告

我們在 Meta 的平台上經營粉絲專頁並刊登廣告，使用自己的工具管理自己的廣告帳戶和粉絲專頁。除了你公開留在我們貼文下的留言，我們從 Meta 和 Steam 只收到彙總的數字，例如廣告的曝光次數、點擊次數和願望清單數量，無法看出是誰看了或點了廣告。你在這些平台上的互動由各平台依其隱私權政策處理，例如 Meta 隱私權政策。

Privacy Policy

Taskbar Miracles: A Little Adventure ・ by CHGameDev ・ Last updated October 21, 2026

What the game does on your computer

  • It only counts presses. The game counts how many keyboard keys and controller buttons you press, so your typing can help the party. It never reads or stores what you type, which keys you press, their order or timing, and it does not know which app you are using. Arrow keys are not counted, and a held key counts once.
  • Fullscreen check. To step aside when you run a fullscreen app, the game checks every 0.5 seconds only whether the front window covers the whole screen. It does not read window titles or app names, and the result is not saved.
  • Pause music when other apps play sound (can be turned off). The game only checks whether another app made a sound in the last second, to pause its own music. It does not record audio or read what is playing or which app played it.
  • Start with Windows (off by default). Only when you turn this on does the game add itself to your Windows startup list; turning it off removes it.
  • Saves stay on your computer. Progress and settings are stored in %APPDATA%\TaskbarMiracles. They hold game state and the key-press total, never your text.
  • No account, no tracking. The game needs no account, contains no analytics or telemetry, and does not send your data to us.

Steam

The game is distributed on Steam, which handles achievements. Your Steam account and purchases are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Steam account data.

Facebook, Instagram, Threads and ads

We run a Page and place ads on Meta's platforms, using our own tool to manage our own ad account and Page. Apart from comments you post publicly under our posts, we only receive aggregate numbers from Meta and Steam, such as ad impressions, clicks and wishlist counts, which cannot tell us who saw or clicked an ad. Your activity on those platforms is handled by each platform under its own policy, such as the Meta Privacy Policy.
